Privacy policy

Effective November 23, 2026

This policy explains what happens to your information when you use this website for Main St Barbershop, 101 S Main St, Suite B, Pomona, CA 91766. It covers this website only.

The short version

  • This site has no accounts, no sign-up forms, no newsletter and no subscriptions.
  • It uses no analytics, no advertising and no tracking pixels.
  • We do not sell or share your personal information, as those terms are defined in California law.
  • The Google map stays off unless you turn it on.

What this site collects

We don't ask you for any personal information on this site. Like almost every website, the company that hosts it may keep standard server logs (such as IP address, browser type, the page requested and the time) to deliver the site and keep it secure. Those logs are not used to identify you or to market to you.

Cookies and local storage

This site does not set its own cookies. It saves a few small items in your browser's storage to remember your choices, so the site doesn't keep asking. They stay on your device and are never sent to us.

Fonts and scripts on this site are served from our own server, so loading a page does not contact third parties.

Global Privacy Control

If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control (GPC) signal, we treat it as a request to opt out of the sale or sharing of your personal information, and we keep optional third-party content off by default.

Booking and calls

Online booking happens on SQUIRE, a separate service. When you book there, SQUIRE collects the details you enter under its own privacy policy (opens in a new tab). The shop receives the appointment details it needs to serve you, such as your name, contact details and the service you booked. If you call us, we use what you tell us only to help you.

Links to other sites

This site links to Google and SQUIRE. Their own privacy policies apply when you visit them.

Your California privacy rights

If you live in California, the California Consumer Privacy Act, as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act, gives you the right to:

  • know what personal information a business collects about you and how it is used and disclosed;
  • ask the business to delete or correct your personal information;
  • opt out of the sale or sharing of your personal information (we don't sell or share it);
  • limit the use of sensitive personal information (we don't collect any through this site);
  • not be discriminated against for using these rights.

To make a request, call (909) 906-8527 or visit the shop. We may need to confirm who you are before we act on a request. You can use an authorized agent to make a request for you.

Children

This site is not directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them through this site.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
