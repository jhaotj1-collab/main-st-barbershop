Accessibility

Last reviewed November 23, 2026

Main St Barbershop wants everyone to be able to use this website. We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at Level AA.

What we've done

  • Every part of the site works with a keyboard, with a clear focus outline and a "Skip to content" link.
  • Pages use real headings, landmarks, lists and tables so screen readers can move around them.
  • Text and buttons meet WCAG AA color contrast.
  • If your device is set to reduce motion, scroll animations are turned off and all content shows right away.
  • Anything that moves on its own, like the scrolling banners and the barber pole, can be stopped with the pause button at the top of the home page. Your choice is remembered. Nothing plays sound.
  • The site works when zoomed to 200% and on small screens.
  • The price estimate, fade picker and barber pole are extras. Everything they show is also available as plain text, and the estimate is announced to screen readers as it changes.
  • Links that open a new tab say so to screen readers.

Content we don't control

Online booking happens on SQUIRE, and the optional map comes from Google. We can't fix accessibility problems on those services. If either one doesn't work for you, call (909) 906-8527 and we'll book you over the phone or give you directions.

Tell us about a problem

If something on this site is hard to use, please let us know. Call (909) 906-8527 or tell us in the shop at 101 S Main St, Suite B, Pomona. Tell us the page and what went wrong, and we'll work to fix it.
