About the shop

A Latino-owned barbershop in downtown Pomona. Skilled barbers, a welcoming chair, and cuts for everyone.

  • Men, women and kidsFades and tapers, women's cuts with blow-dry, and kids' cuts.
  • New look? Take your timeBook the new client consultation and get extra time to talk through your hair and style.
  • 5+ years in businessRight downtown, in Suite B at 101 S Main St.
